# India Reference Layer

Useful reference families for later knowledge-base expansion:
- General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 and current amendments.
- Department of Expenditure procurement manuals for Goods, Works and Non-Consultancy Services.
- GST, e-invoicing and e-way-bill requirements where applicable.
- MSE and Make-in-India procurement policies where the buyer is subject to them.

Important: government procurement rules should not be silently treated as mandatory for a private company. Applicability depends on the buyer and transaction.
